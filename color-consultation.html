Understanding Light Temperature & Wall Pigments

North-Facing Rooms

Receives cool, bluish daylight. We recommend warm ochres, terracotta, or champagne ivories to balance cold shadows and prevent a dingy cast.

South-Facing Rooms

Bathed in warm, intense all-day light. Accommodates cooler sage greens, slate blues, or deep naval hues without feeling chilly or dark.

Artificial LED (2700K vs 4000K)

Our color consultants test physical sample boards under both daytime sunlight and your specific nighttime indoor lighting fixtures.
