Our Story, Craftsmanship & Architectural Heritage

Dedicated to transforming architectural spaces through master craftsmanship, sustainable mineral chemistry, certified expertise, and an uncompromising commitment to perfection.

Our Heritage

Founded On Precision, Built On Integrity

Established in 2014 in New York, Aura Finishes & Co. began with a clear conviction: that painting is not merely a surface coating, but the vital architectural element defining mood, light, and character in every luxury home and commercial space.

Over the past decade, our master artisans have restored historic brownstones, executed Level-5 finishes for Manhattan penthouses, and applied state-of-the-art acoustic wall systems for international luxury brands and private estates.

Craftsmanship Philosophy

We Don’t Just Paint Walls. We Create Lasting Atmosphere.

Every stroke, trowel pass, and pigment formulation is calculated to harmonize with architecture, natural lighting, and interior lifestyle.

Light Diffusion Science

We calibrate paint sheens and mineral micro-textures specifically to absorb, soften, or bounce natural light across ceilings and architectural contours.

Dustless HEPA Prep

German Festool HEPA extraction sanders eliminate 99.7% of airborne particulate matter, ensuring pristine air quality and smooth substrate perfection.

Color Precision Match

Digital spectrophotometer analysis guarantees 100% hue accuracy, reproducing bespoke designer swatch formulations without color deviation.

Level-5 Quality Standard

Full continuous skim coating, crack mesh reinforcement, and triple raking-light inspection guarantee zero flashing, roller marks, or seams.

Materials & Techniques

Authentic Mineral Formulations & Classical Artistry

We exclusively specify ultra-premium, durable, and sustainable materials sourced from world-class artisanal manufacturers and Italian quarries:

Italian Marmorino
Fine Carrara marble aggregate and slaked lime from Treviso for luminous monolithic sheen.
Organic Limewash
Breathable, antimicrobial potassium mineral coatings for soft cloud-troweled depth.
Elastomeric Membranes
300% flexible acrylic-siloxane weather-shields engineered for harsh climate extremes.
Hand-Laid 23k Gold Leaf
Master gilding for ornate moldings, boutique niches, and statement architectural accents.
