Privacy Policy

Your trust and confidentiality are paramount. Learn how Aura Finishes & Co. collects, secures, and handles your personal and property data.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you engage with Aura Finishes & Co. for painting estimates, color consultations, or contracted surface applications, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Details: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and physical estate address.
  • Project Specifics: Architectural drawings, square footage dimensions, surface substrates, desired finish levels, and photo references.
  • Client Portal Accounts: Login credentials, project milestone updates, invoice records, and warranty logs.
  • Technical Data: Browser user-agent, IP address, and cookie identifiers utilized exclusively for theme persistence (dark/light mode) and secure session authentication.

2. How We Use Your Data

All collected information is processed solely for legitimate contracting and client-care objectives:

  • Preparing transparent cost proposals and precision material calculations.
  • Coordinating on-site artisan access, safety protocols, and dustless containment setup.
  • Issuing official certificate warranties upon milestone completion.
  • Sending optional seasonal architectural palette guides (which you may unsubscribe from at any time).

3. Zero Third-Party Monetization & Confidentiality

Strict Non-Disclosure: We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize client records, floor plans, or contact info to third-party marketing firms or data brokers.

Project photos and case study transformations are only published on our public portfolio after explicit, signed authorization from the property owner or representative architect.

4. Data Protection & Security Standards

We employ rigorous physical, organizational, and technological safeguards to shield your sensitive information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All electronic records are stored on encrypted servers with TLS 1.3 protocol standards.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You retain full ownership of your personal data. At any time, you may request:

  • A complete digital copy of all personal and project data stored with us.
  • Correction or updates to your contact records and billing preferences.
  • Permanent deletion of your account and archived proposal submissions (subject to legal accounting retention obligations).

Have Questions Regarding Our Privacy Policies?

Our privacy compliance officer and client relations team are available to answer any questions or process data requests.
