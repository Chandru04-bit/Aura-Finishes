The Aura Standard

Our 6-Stage Execution Process

Flawless wall finishing requires disciplined methodology. Here is how we guarantee perfection on every square foot.

01

Initial Consultation

We discuss your aesthetic vision, functional requirements, lighting parameters, and schedule preferences.

02

3D Site Inspection

Laser precision room measurement, moisture testing, substrate analysis, and transparent cost quotation.

03

Color & Finish Curation

On-wall physical sample boards, texture swatch approvals, and custom pigment formulation.

04

Dustless Preparation

Total furniture masking, floor armor installation, HEPA micro-sanding, and premium primer application.

05

Master Application

Multi-coat artisan application using German airless technology or hand-troweled Venetian plaster.

06

Final White-Glove Audit

High-lumen raking light inspection, thorough site clean-up, and 10-year warranty certificate handover.
